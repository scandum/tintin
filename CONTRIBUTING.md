# Contributing to TinTin++

TinTin++ is primarily maintained as a solo project. Read the guidelines before trying to contribute.

---

## 💬 Discuss Before You Code

Because TinTin++ is a project with specific design goals it is generally best to discuss your planned contributions first. Unsolicited, large pull requests may be difficult to integrate.

Before opening a Pull Request:
* Join the TinTin++ Discord linked from [tintin.mudhalla.net](https://tintin.mudhalla.net).
* Share your ideas in the appropriate channel to ensure they align with the general roadmap.

## ✏️ Design goals

1. Avoid bloat.
2. High performance.
3. Quality engineering.
4. Simple but not stupid.
